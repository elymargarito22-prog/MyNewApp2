Questions, answered

How far ahead should I order?

As early as you can, especially around graduation season. Tell us your event date in your request and we'll confirm the timing before we start.

Can I make changes to my design?

Yes. We share your design with you first and tweak it until it feels right. Nothing is made until you approve it.

Do you make orders for groups or whole classes?

Absolutely. Party labels by the dozen, matching stoles for a group, caps for friends graduating together. Include the quantity in your request and we'll put together a quote.

Can I get my order quickly?

Sometimes. Send your request with your date and we'll let you know honestly whether we can make it in time.

How do I get my order?

Let us know where you are when you reach out and we'll go over the pickup and delivery options for your order.

How much does it cost?

Every piece is custom, so pricing depends on the design, materials and quantity. Send us your idea and we'll reply with a quote, with no obligation.

Let's make something you'll love.

Tell us a little about your event. We'll reach out to talk ideas, timing and pricing.
